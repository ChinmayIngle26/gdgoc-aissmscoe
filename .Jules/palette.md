## 2024-05-28 - Avoid copy-paste errors and make Framer Motion hover animations accessible
**Learning:** Components created by copying and pasting similar components often retain the original's accessibility attributes (like `aria-label` or `alt` text), which can lead to confusing and incorrect screen reader announcements (e.g., a LinkedIn button announcing itself as a Whatsapp button). Additionally, Framer Motion animations triggered only by `whileHover` exclude keyboard users from experiencing the intended interaction.
**Action:** Always manually verify `aria-label` and `alt` attributes on newly created or copied components. Ensure they accurately reflect the component's specific function. For Framer Motion animations, always pair `whileHover` with `whileFocus` to ensure the interaction is natively accessible to keyboard users navigating via Tab.
