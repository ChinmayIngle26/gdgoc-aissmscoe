## 2024-05-24 - Floating Social Overlays & Animated Focus States
**Learning:** Fixed flex containers used to group interactive overlays (like floating social buttons) can inadvertently block clicks on the underlying page content if the container itself has a solid invisible bounding box. Additionally, decorative icons inside interactive components with screen reader text need `aria-hidden` and empty `alt` text to prevent redundant announcements, and Framer Motion hover animations must be paired with `whileFocus` for keyboard accessibility.
**Action:** Apply `pointer-events-none` to the container and `*:pointer-events-auto` to its children. Always pair `whileHover` with `whileFocus` on Framer Motion elements, add `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none`, use `shrink-0` on internal flex images to prevent squashing during width animations, and apply `aria-hidden="true" alt=""` to decorative child images.
