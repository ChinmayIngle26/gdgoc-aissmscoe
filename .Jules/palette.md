## 2025-04-06 - Social Link Keyboard Accessibility & Copy-Paste Fixes
**Learning:** Found multiple floating social buttons using `framer-motion` that lacked `whileFocus` counterparts to their `whileHover` expanding animations, making the extra context completely inaccessible to keyboard users. Also found copy-paste errors in `aria-label`s and `alt` tags between identical button components. Decorative images within `aria-label` links should be hidden to avoid screen reader redundancy.
**Action:** Always pair `whileHover` with `whileFocus` in Framer Motion for interactive elements. Double-check `aria-label` accuracy when copying components, and consistently use `alt="" aria-hidden="true"` on purely decorative inner icons. Always ensure links opening externally have `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
