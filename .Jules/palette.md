## 2023-11-25 - Framer Motion Accessibility\n**Learning:** When using `whileHover` in Framer Motion, animations are completely inaccessible to keyboard users because hover does not trigger on focus.\n**Action:** Always pair `whileHover` with `whileFocus` to ensure animations are accessible. Combine this with Tailwind `focus-visible` classes (like `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none`) to provide clear focus indicators for screen readers and keyboard users.
