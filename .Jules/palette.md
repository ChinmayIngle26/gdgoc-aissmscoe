## 2026-04-12 - Framer Motion Keyboard Accessibility and ARIA Label Copy-Paste Risk
**Learning:** Components heavily reliant on Framer Motion's `whileHover` prop for visual interaction hide critical interactive states from keyboard users if not paired properly. Additionally, identical interactive components (like floating social buttons) share structural similarities, increasing the risk of developer copy-paste errors (e.g., a LinkedIn button carrying a 'Join Whatsapp Group' ARIA label).
**Action:** When building interactive Framer Motion components, strictly pair `whileHover` with `whileFocus` (and combine with Tailwind `focus-visible` utility classes for clear focus rings). Always manually review `aria-label` attributes on repeated components to ensure they accurately reflect the specific component's function.
