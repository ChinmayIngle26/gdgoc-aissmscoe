## 2024-05-18 - Floating Social Buttons Animation Accessibility & Copy-Paste Artifacts
**Learning:** Framer Motion interactions like `whileHover` do not automatically trigger for keyboard users. They must be explicitly paired with `whileFocus`. Furthermore, copy-pasting component templates (like duplicating DiscordButton to WhatsappButton and LinkedinButton) frequently leads to lingering, inaccurate ARIA labels and `alt` text that severely degrade the screen reader experience.
**Action:** Always verify keyboard accessibility of hover animations by adding `whileFocus`. When duplicating UI components, meticulously audit `aria-label`, `alt` attributes, and comments to ensure they accurately reflect the new component's intent. Hide purely decorative SVG/img elements inside interactive wrappers with `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`.
