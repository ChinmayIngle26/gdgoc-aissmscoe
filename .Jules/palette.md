## 2024-05-24 - Floating Interactive Button Groups
**Learning:** Hardcoding `absolute right-0 bottom-X` on individual floating buttons inside a common container causes accessibility and layout issues when animations (like expanding width on hover) trigger. Screen reader focus order may not match visual order, and focus transitions (like `whileFocus` in Framer Motion) get skipped. Copy-pasting also led to incorrect `aria-label`s on buttons (LinkedIn had WhatsApp label).
**Action:** When grouping floating interactive elements, use a fixed flex container with `flex-col items-end` and rely on normal document flow within it. Remove internal `absolute` positioning. Add `focus-visible:ring-2` and pair `whileHover` with `whileFocus` for keyboard accessibility, and ensure links to external platforms use `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
